## 3.2 Scope, Non-Goals & Decisions

`E1-M1` authorizes an isolated, reversible shootout between the OpenIE entity-state HMM (`yaw/daemon/graph/ppr.py`, `retriever_mode="graph"`, default) and the zero-OpenIE **Kraus Sheaf Sub-Span HMM with Myerson Coalitional Reranking** (`yaw/daemon/graph/hmm.py`, `retriever_mode="manifold"`), plus Pass-3 operator remediations, Pass-4 structural engine healing (`spaCy` `en_core_web_sm` `doc.sents`, `token.is_stop`, `token.lemma_`, `doc.noun_chunks` prose segmentation & lexical/anaphora graph construction deleting `131`-line `_STOPWORDS`, `"manifold-v2"` de-titled `subspan_vec`, relaxed CSLS dense bridges, and non-negative matched `chunk_fts`/`vec0` external hints leveraging existing OpenIE AST/MaxP hydration), build-sorted `np.add.reduceat` scanline reductions, the **5-Parameter Hydrodynamic / RMT / Null-Sketch Reduction** (`yaw.toml` `[manifold]` cleared `{}` with zero Optuna knob-twisting sweeps), single-study Optuna `5.0.0` `TPESampler` (MOTPE + c-TPE) `5`-point `Recall@{1, 2, 5, 10, 20}` diagnostics (`yaw/daemon/manifold_optuna.py`), and benchmark horizon alignment (`yaw/daemon/runtime.py`, `benchmark.py`).

### Five Mathematical & Architectural Root Causes + Scatter Bottleneck (`63c9c00, dirty`)

1. **Lexical-Bridge Sheaf Transport Collapse & Missing Symmetric Balancing (`hmm.py:_build_heterogeneous_stalks` `L604-606`, `_compute_chunk_connections` `L1216`, `build_substate_manifold` `L1456-1486`, `63c9c00, dirty`):** On cached DBs (`[measured: scripts/probe_manifold.py -> outputs/benchmarks/probe_manifold_pre_pass3.json, 63c9c00, dirty]`), multi-gold chunk pairs are `223/224` (`99.55%` on `offset=10`, `80/80` queries any-pair / `79/80` all-pairs) and `148/148` (`100%` on `offset=0`, `40/40` queries) reachable within `2` hops (`197/224 = 87.95%` direct 1-hop on `offset=10` with `184/224` `lexical_only` 1-hop & `66/224` `dense_assisted` 1-hop; `142/148 = 95.95%` direct 1-hop on `offset=0` with `130/148` `lexical_only` 1-hop & `48/148` `dense_assisted` 1-hop). Yet `136,666` lexical-only edges on `offset=10` retain mean $\|T_e u_{\text{src}}\|^2 = 0.1176$ (`119,305/136,666 = 87.30% < 0.25`) vs. `5,912` dense-assisted edges at mean `0.6360` (`415/5,912 = 7.02% < 0.25`); `offset=0` exhibits lexical-only mean `0.0918` across `61,169` edges (`56,070/61,169 = 91.66% < 0.25`) vs. `2,650` dense-assisted edges at mean `0.5719` (`420/2,650 = 15.85% < 0.25`). Unrescued orthogonal projection $T_e \rho T_e^\top$ discards ~`90%` `[observed: scripts/probe_manifold.py -> outputs/benchmarks/probe_manifold_pre_pass3.json, 63c9c00, dirty]` of cross-domain lexical bridge trace mass. Additionally, `_build_heterogeneous_stalks` (`L604-606`, `63c9c00, dirty`) truncates own-span modes to `ceil(eff_rank_sib)` (`0/899` chunks shrunk on `offset=10` and `0/449` on `offset=0` `[measured: scripts/probe_manifold.py -> outputs/benchmarks/probe_manifold_pre_pass3.json, 63c9c00, dirty]` — benign on tiny slices, but risks silent mode loss on larger skewed chunks), and `default_inter_csr` is row-stochastic only without Sinkhorn–Knopp hub-sink balancing.
2. **Duplicate `apply_kraus_intra` & Early Hop Cutoff (`hmm.py:_evolve_kraus_particle` `L2045, L2084`, `ManifoldHyperparams.num_hops=3` `L211`, `63c9c00, dirty`):** `_evolve_kraus_particle` calls `apply_kraus_intra` BEFORE inter-chunk transport (`L2045`) and AGAIN immediately AFTER inter-chunk transport (`L2084`), diluting freshly landed target sub-spans into siblings (`K_intra^2` stacking across hops) prior to Lüders measurement, and relies on `num_hops = 3` (`hw_tail_all_zero_queries` `8/80` on `offset=10` and `10/40` on `offset=0`, `hw_tail_all_lt_0_20_queries` `9/20` and `hw_tail_any_lt_0_20_queries` `17/20` on `offset=10` `PopQA` `[measured: scripts/probe_manifold.py -> outputs/benchmarks/probe_manifold_pre_pass3.json, 63c9c00, dirty]`). Meanwhile, `_evolve_kraus_particle` must **retain** both orthogonal visit gates (`eff_obs = obs_score * unvis_sub` at `L2172` and `l_next = (params.null_likelihood_floor + ...) * novelty_sub` at `L2182-2187`) because dropping `novelty_sub` from `l_next` leaks `null_likelihood_floor = 0.08` (`50x` above `0.0016`) on visited chunks.
3. **Sparse External Seed `_robust_z` Saturation (`hmm.py:run_substate_hmm` `L2751-2757`, `63c9c00, dirty`):** Dense zero-padding over `chunk_ids` before `_robust_z` produces `median = 0.0` and `MAD = 0.0` whenever fewer than `50%` `[observed: scripts/probe_manifold.py -> outputs/benchmarks/probe_manifold_pre_pass3.json, 63c9c00, dirty]` of chunks match (`80/80` FTS5 queries on `offset=10`, `40/40` on `offset=0`, and `20/80` `vec0` queries where `k=200 < 0.5 * C` for `C=418` on `musique`, `20/20 = 100%` `[measured: scripts/probe_manifold.py -> outputs/benchmarks/probe_manifold_pre_pass3.json, 63c9c00, dirty]`), forcing MAD scale to `z_ext_floor = 0.10` and saturating distinct ranks against `external_z_max = 6.0`.
4. **Hop-0 Erosion, Starved Ancestors, Pool-32 Clipping & Joinless `B=2` Mixing (`hmm.py:run_substate_hmm` `L2764-2909`, `_myerson_coalitional_rerank` `L2436-2513`, `63c9c00, dirty`):** Multi-particle inference caps $B \le 2$ (`max_particles = 2`) and merges branches via scalar chunk-level Noisy-OR + degree PMI without sub-span tensor Join BP contraction across disjoint query branches; `_myerson_coalitional_rerank` truncates candidates at `myerson_pool_m = 32` (`gold_doc_gt32_hop0_posterior = 14/174` matched gold docs, `default_hop0_only = 15/174`, `gold_doc_gt32_final = 4/174` on `offset=10` `[measured: scripts/probe_manifold.py -> outputs/benchmarks/probe_manifold_pre_pass3.json, 63c9c00, dirty]`), blocks Steiner dividend flow whenever `b_u <= 0.0` (`L2437`) or above `min(b_u, ...)` (`L2440`), and scales `b_val` multiplicatively without protecting top Hop-0 anchors (`default` `R@1 = 0.4000` vs. raw `vec_hits R@1 = 0.4406` `[measured: scripts/probe_manifold.py -> outputs/benchmarks/probe_manifold_pre_pass3.json, 63c9c00, dirty]`).
5. **Unbuffered `np.add.at` Scatter Bottleneck (`hmm.py:_apply_kraus_inter_core` `L1632, L1636`, `apply_kraus_intra` `L1698`, `apply_kraus_intra_adjoint` `L1723`, `63c9c00, dirty`):** Four unbuffered per-edge scatter-add loops serialize random writes instead of using contiguous `np.add.reduceat` scanlines over build-sorted `(dst, src)` edge arrays.

### Pass-3 Post-Shootout Structural Root Causes (`P4-1`–`P4-5`, `63c9c00, dirty`)

1. **`P4-1` — AST Code Sub-Span Splitting & Redundant Title-Stamped Sub-Span Embeddings (`yaw/daemon/ingestion/corpus.py:491-525`, `yaw/daemon/indexing.py:158-162`, `yaw/daemon/graph/inference/embedding.py:503-516`, `63c9c00, dirty`):**

3. **`P4-3` — HotpotQA Multi-Hop `-0.10` `Recall@2` Regression (`0.875 -> 0.775`) via Unlemmatized Hop-1+ Diffusion & Comparison Anchor Erosion (`hmm.py:run_substate_hmm`, `probe_manifold_pass3_final.json:462-529`, `63c9c00, dirty`):**

## 3.6 Design (Pass-3 Canonical Target State)

### Tree/DAG Sheaf Tensor-Network & Build-Sorted Scanline Architecture

Grounded in the Sheaf Tensor-Network & Amiga Agnus/Blitter scanline analysis (from interactive design analysis, 2026-11-14), Pass-3 casts multi-particle sheaf belief propagation as an explicit **Tree/DAG Sheaf Tensor Network** with **build-sorted scanline `np.add.reduceat` segmented reductions**:

```
  [Hop 0 Clarity Seeding] --> B <= max_particles (3) disjoint sub-span anchors
          |
          v  (for each branch b in 0..B-1)
  P_h[b, s, :, :]  (4-tensor: particle x subspan x r x r PSD density)
          |
          +--> [K_intra once per hop (pre-inter)] --> [Stalk Luders D_c]
          |
          +--> [K_inter via 3-tensor T_e = U_{dst}^T U_{src} + linear CPTP rescue]
          |       \--> segmented sum over build-sorted (inter_dst, inter_src) via np.add.reduceat
          |
          +--> [Residual Dense/Lex Score + Orthogonal Unvisited / Null-Floor Novelty Gates + Fischer-Ramdas dLL_{h+1}]
          +--> [Bilinear Luders Measurement L_{h+1} rho L_{h+1}^T]
          +--> [Exact Linear Adjoint Backward Sweep: D_op^* (K_inter^* (L^* B_{h+1} L))]
          |
```

## 4. Epistemic Grounding, Empirical Ablations & Post-`E1-M1` Roadmap

### 4.1 Epistemic Grounding — Hidden States & Tensor-Network Belief Math

- **Rejected Claims:** Static corpora do not evolve over wall-clock time, and simplex-normalized cosine/IDF² potentials are uncalibrated belief states rather than frequentist factual truth probabilities (`[hmm-retrieval.md](hmm-retrieval.md)`).
- **Physical Model:** Per Miller, Leek & Schwartz (SIGIR 1999) and Lafferty & Zhai (2001), latent variable $Z_h$ represents the **unobserved compositional query referent state at hop $h$** while corpus sub-spans are polysemous surface emissions sharing strings across disjoint referents. Continuous heterogeneous stalks $U_c \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times r_c}$ and PSD density 4-tensors $\mathcal{P}_h[b, s] \succeq 0$ evolve multi-sense superposition coherently along DAG paths (`O(|E| \cdot H)` sum-product forward-backward smoothing), attenuating dead-end hubs that fail downstream measurement constraints without destructive signed-vector path cancellation.

### 4.2 Expected Performance Regimes & Primary Structural Ceiling

Zero-LLM indexing eliminates serial OpenIE latency (`41.5398x` `[measured: shootout_4ds_10q_initial.json, 63c9c00, dirty]` slice speedup). Primary structural ceiling remains cross-sentence pronoun/definite-NP coreference paired with non-lexical world-knowledge aliases on `MuSiQue`: whenever neither `P_den` nor `P_lex` contains an edge, downstream Kraus/Myerson algebra cannot fabricate the missing link.

### 4.3 Mandatory Kill-Switch Falsification Ablations (`WP5`)

1. **Backward Adjoint Kill-Switch (`B_h = I`):** Disabling backward sweeps must degrade `2WikiMultiHopQA` / `MuSiQue` `Recall@5` by `>= 0.02 [target]`.
2. **Stalk Rank Kill-Switch (`dim` scalar rank-1 vs. heterogeneous `r_c`):** Multi-rank stalks + Lüders deflation must outperform scalar rank-1 belief propagation on polysemous bridge tests.
3. **Dataset-Blind Hop Gate (`dLL_1` Separation):** Single-hop `PopQA` `hop_weights[1]` mean stays `< 0.15 [target]` while multi-hop chains (`HotpotQA`, `2WikiMultiHopQA`, `MuSiQue`) achieve `hop_weights[1]` mean `>= 0.35 [target]` (clearing `route_min_gate`).
4. **CPTP Rescue & Join BP Kill-Switches:** Paired ablation of Source-Starved rescue vs. `0.0` (`>= 0.015 [target]` `Recall@5` margin on multi-hop lexical bridges) and `coalition_coupling = 0.0` vs. `0.45` (`>= 0.015 [target]` `Recall@5` margin on multi-branch join queries) measured cleanly against `5`-pt retrieval recall.
