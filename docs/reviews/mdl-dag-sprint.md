## Part 1: Post-Mortem on Mock Harness, Math Shortcomings & Empirical `3cd3952` Defects

### §1.1 Four Artificial Pathologies in Earlier Mock-Embedding Research
The synthetic mock harness used during initial Rev-1 design exhibited four pathologies absent from real `EmbeddingGemma` embeddings:
1. **i.i.d. Gaussian vectors with endpoint juking:** Hop-3 answer tokens were placed directly inside `query_text`, masking broken multi-hop traversal.
2. **Disconnected distractor embeddings:** Exact query predicate tags (`weight = 0.82`) were injected into generic boilerplate embeddings whose text did not contain those predicates, creating artificial super-decoys.
3. **Zero cosine across orthogonal hash tags:** Hash vectors gave $\mathbb{E}[\langle e_{\text{born\_in}}, e_{\text{birthplace}} \rangle] = 0$, whereas real embeddings share semantic subspace correlation.
4. **Degenerate 3-vector background spectrum:** Reusing only three background tags across 180 chunks collapsed participation ratio $D_{\text{eff}}$ to `9.7–15.0` and injected a `+0.50` baseline cosine offset.

### §1.2 Six Genuine Mathematical & Engineering Shortcomings (Rev-2 Findings)
1. **Stage-1 `RaBitQ` unused at Hops 1–2:** Scanning only Wave 0 via `RaBitQ` blinded Waves 1–2 to non-lexical paraphrases, acronyms, and saturated `df = 8` lexical bridges; fixed via **step-wise spherical partial correlation controlling for $\hat{q}$** ($\rho_{uv\cdot q}$).
2. **Four Rev-1 Gibbs objective errors:** Double-counted Hop-0 facet + semantic potentials (`log_bf` miscalibration), terminal-only `node_resid_nats` (stopping early at Hop 0 on paraphrases), row-normalized bridge dilution (`w_cond / row_w` erasing bridge MI), and same-branch negative-bridge QR deflation (penalizing true Hop-2 targets; QR deflation is strictly for cross-turn `exclude_chunk_ids`).
3. **`FTS5 tokenize='trigram'` misses 2-char CJK tokens:** Verified in SQLite `3.53.1`: contentless trigram `MATCH` returns `0` rows on all `< 3`-char CJK words (`鲁迅`, `绍兴`, `苏轼`, `眉山`, `海思`, `杜甫`). Resolved via `chunk_vocab` $O(\log V)$ B-tree range seek expanded into `MATCH` OR-clauses plus Church–Hanks Positive PMI.
4. **Mid-word infix slicing, `ß <-> ss` trigram casefolding & space-gram blindness (`EN` & `DE`):** Character-only watershed sliced mid-word infixes (`pub[lish]ed`), failed on sibling German compounds (`Nordkanaldienst` $\leftrightarrow$ `Nordkanalverwaltung`), missed umlauts / `ß <-> ss` (because SQLite FTS5 `tokenize='trigram'` applies 1-to-1 Unicode case folding only, not full `str.casefold()`, unless contentless FTS5 rows are normalized via `.casefold()` at index time), and discarded space-spanning trigrams (`"Red Sea"`, `"Bo Wu"`). Resolved via left-anchored `.casefold()` stems paired with `.casefold()` contentless FTS5 indexing, Koehn–Knight symmetric decompounding, and Positive-PMI 2-word collocations.
5. **Quadratic $D_{\text{eff}}$ collapse & `4 * k_c` BnB cap:** Replaced Rényi-2 participation ratio with Shannon spectral entropy dimension $D_{\text{eff}} = \exp(-\sum p_i \ln p_i)$ and replaced the static `4 * k_c` BnB cap with Two-Phase Progressive BnB.
6. **`pathlib`, `PRAGMA query_only`, SoA RAM & pool safety:** Replaced `fnmatch` with separator-aware `PurePosixPath.full_match`, eliminated sidecar `.yaw/stage1/` files in favor of single-file `yaw_stage1`, switched `RaBitQ` to SoA `(W, N)` (`~108 B/chunk`), and avoided `PRAGMA query_only` DDL crashes.

### §1 Information-Theoretic Formulation, Frontier Hypotheses (`H1`–`H4`) & Recall Funnel

Let $N = |V_{\text{corpus}}|$, $\hat{x}_i = \frac{x_i - \mu}{\|x_i - \mu\|_2}$, and $D_{\text{eff}} = \exp\!\big(-\sum_j p_j \ln p_j\big)$ where $p_j = \lambda_j / \sum_m \lambda_m$ are normalized eigenvalues of the `2,048`-sample centered covariance matrix, with fixed structural scales (`horizon = 3`, wave cap `12`, clone Jaccard threshold `3/4` so $\text{novelty} \ge 0.25$):

1. **Structural Scales:** $d_{\text{bridge}} = \max(4, \lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil)$, $d_{\text{query}} = \max(8, \lfloor N / 2 \rfloor)$, $f_{\max} = \min(6, \max(2, \lfloor \frac{1}{2}\log_2 \max(4, N) \rfloor))$, $|C_T| \le 3 d_{\text{bridge}}$.
2. **Multilingual Anchor Extraction (`main.chunk_vocab` or `temp.chunk_vocab` via `fts5vocab(main, chunk_fts, 'row')` + casefolded boundary-padded `chunk_fts`):**
   - **Word-anchored stems & Koehn–Knight decompounding:** Query text is normalized with `.casefold()` to match casefolded `chunk_fts`/`chunk_vocab` entries (`ß <-> ss` symmetric!). Extracts left-anchored prefixes $w[:L]$ ($L \ge 4$) at DF transitions/suffix seams + symmetric decompounding $(w[:a], w[a:])$ ($a \ge 4, |w|-a \ge 4$) verified via $\min(\text{df}(w[:a]), \text{df}(w[a:])) > \text{df}(w)$ and boundary-padded word-start `" " + w[a:]` in `chunk_fts`.
   - **Short non-CJK words (`<= 3` chars):** Exact boundary-padded trigram lookups (`f" {w} "` for len `1..2`, `f" {w}"` / `w` for len `3`) so acronyms/numbers (`AI`, `Go`, `UK`, `US`, `id`, `20`, `404`) are first-class lexical facets without stopword lists.
   - **2-word collocations:** Adjacent words `"w1 w2"` filtered by Church–Hanks Positive PMI: $N \cdot \text{df}(w_1 w_2) \ge 2\,\text{df}(w_1)\,\text{df}(w_2)$.
   - **Unspaced CJK 2-char & 3/4-char anchors:** Trailing-padded `chunk_fts` makes EOF 2-char CJK (`'碎叶 '`) $O(\log V)$ B-tree seekable alongside `'碎叶*'`; 3/4-char spans filtered by Positive PMI against straddling bigrams.
3. **Stage-1 SoA `RaBitQ`, Two-Phase Progressive BnB & Step-Wise Partial Correlation:**
   - Unbiased cosine estimate $\hat{c}(u, v)$ with high-dimensional concentration half-width $\varepsilon_i = \frac{\sqrt{1-s_i^2}}{s_i}\sqrt{\frac{2\ln\max(2, N)}{D - 1}}$; Progressive BnB executes exact `WHERE chunk_id = ?` point fetches until lower/upper bounds certify exact top-$k_c$.
   - Across Waves 1–2, `RaBitQ` scans top frontier vectors $\hat{u}$ for spherical partial correlation orthogonal to $\hat{q}$:
     $$\rho_{uv \cdot q} = \frac{\hat{c}(u, v) - \hat{c}(u, q)\,\hat{c}(q, v)}{\sqrt{1 - \hat{c}(u, q)^2}\,\sqrt{1 - \hat{c}(q, v)^2}}$$
     admitting semantic bridges exceeding Bonferroni surprise $I_{\text{null}}(\rho_{uv\cdot q}; D_{\text{eff}} - 1) > \ln(N + 1)$ alongside FTS5 lexical bridges ranked by joint query + bridge surprisal.
4. **Exact `L <= 3` Simple-Path Stopped Gibbs ($\tau \equiv 1.0$) & NNLS Cone Completeness:**
   For every **simple** path $P = (v_0 \to \dots \to v_L)$ ($0 \le L \le 3$, $v_i \neq v_j$ for $i \neq j$) enumerated or prefix-state evaluated over the bounded candidate subgraph $|C_T| \le 3 d_{\text{bridge}}$ with union facet mask $m_L = \bigcup_{t=0}^L \text{mask}(v_t)$:
   $$\log W(P) = \sum_{b \in m_L} I(f_b) + r(v_0) + \sum_{t=0}^{L-1} \big(1 - \max(0, \langle \hat{v}_t, \hat{v}_{t+1}\rangle)^2\big)\,r(v_{t+1}) - \left(\ln(N+1) + \sum_{t=0}^{L-1} c_{\text{hop}}(v_t \to v_{t+1}) - \ln\rho_{\text{stop}}(v_L)\right)$$
   eliminating 2-cycle and 3-cycle bounces exactly (unlike `(v, mask)` walk DP in `A4_WALK_GIBBS`), paired with exact simple-path posterior marginals `node_post`/`e_occ`, log Bayes factor $\ln Z / Z_0$, and nat-scaled NNLS cone completeness:
   $$\text{completeness}(C) = \frac{\sum_{b \in m(C)} I(f_b) + \min\!\big(I_{\text{null}}(\|\mathbf{V}_{\text{comp}}\mathbf{w}^+\|_2; D_{\text{eff}}),\, \ln(N+2)\big)}{\sum_b I(f_b) + \ln(N+2)}.$$

#### Preregistered Tier B Frontier Discovery Extensions (`H1`–`H4`) & 3-Stage Diagnostic Funnel
Every Tier B discovery query logs **3-Stage Recall Funnel Telemetry**:
$$\text{All-Hops}@5 = P(\text{Hop0\_Hit}) \times P(\text{Subgraph\_AllHops} \mid \text{Hop0\_Hit}) \times \text{Rank\_Efficiency},$$
diagnosing whether failures occur in Hop-0 seeding, Wave 1–2 bridge acquisition, or simple-path MDL top-$5$ ranking. Four **parameter-free** mathematical hypotheses are tested on Tier B (`EVAL-2a`..`EVAL-2c`) while Tier C stays sealed:
- **`H1` — Marchenko–Pastur Bulk Whitening (RMT):** Project out eigenvalues strictly above the MP upper bulk edge $\lambda_+ = \sigma^2(1 + \sqrt{D/N})^2$ prior to `RaBitQ` & $\rho_{uv\cdot q}$ to suppress real `EmbeddingGemma` rogue anisotropy spikes.
- **`H2` — Multi-Branch Set-Level MDL Decoding:** Augment linear MAP decoding with submodular pair/set completion over disconnected parallel evidence chains ($A \parallel B$) for comparison/intersection queries (`2Wiki`, `HotpotQA`).
- **`H3` — Exact `L <= 3` Simple-Path Gibbs vs. `(v, mask)` Walk DP (`A4_WALK_GIBBS`):** Isolate exact empirical gain of eliminating 2-/3-cycle leakage on `log_bf`, `Rank_Efficiency`, and `Reject AUROC`.
- **`H4` — Empirical Background Cosine Tail Calibration:** Compare parametric Beta null $I_{\text{null}}(c; D_{\text{eff}})$ vs. empirical quantile tail nats measured on the `2,048`-sample matrix.
